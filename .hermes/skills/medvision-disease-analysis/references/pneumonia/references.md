# references.md — Pneumonia/CAP Disease Analysis v1

## S1 — ATS/IDSA CAP guideline 2019
Metlay JP, Waterer GW, Long AC, et al. **Diagnosis and Treatment of Adults with Community-acquired Pneumonia. An Official Clinical Practice Guideline of the American Thoracic Society and Infectious Diseases Society of America.** Am J Respir Crit Care Med. 2019;200(7):e45-e67. PMID 31573350. PMCID PMC6812437. DOI 10.1164/rccm.201908-1581ST.
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6812437/

Use for:
- adult CAP scope;
- pneumonia syndrome with radiographic confirmation in the guideline population;
- clinical features alone are insufficiently accurate;
- severe CAP = 1 major OR >=3 minor criteria;
- low procalcitonin must not independently erase otherwise compatible evidence.

## S2 — IDSA CAP Clinical Pathway
**Community-Acquired Pneumonia (CAP) Clinical Pathway.** Infectious Diseases Society of America.
URL: https://www.idsociety.org/globalassets/idsa/practice-guidelines/community-acquired-pneumonia-in-adults/cap-clinical-pathway-final-online.pdf

Use for:
- CAP means pneumonia acquired outside hospital;
- major immunocompromising conditions are outside pathway scope;
- disease/severity reasoning is contextual.

## S3 — ACR Appropriateness Criteria
**ACR Appropriateness Criteria® Acute Respiratory Illness in Immunocompetent Patients.** Revised 2024.
URL: https://acsearch.acr.org/docs/69446/Narrative/

Use for:
- chest radiography in acute respiratory illness;
- negative/indeterminate CXR does not universally close pneumonia evaluation when clinical concern remains;
- MedVision must not autonomously order CT.

## S4 — Fleischner Society Glossary 2024
Bankier AA, MacMahon H, Colby T, et al. **Fleischner Society: Glossary of Terms for Thoracic Imaging.** Radiology. 2024. PMID 38411514. PMCID PMC10902601. DOI 10.1148/radiol.232558.
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10902601/

Use for:
- consolidation/opacity are imaging descriptors;
- morphology must not be converted directly to infectious etiology.
