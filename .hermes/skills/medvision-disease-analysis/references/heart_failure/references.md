# references.md — Heart Failure / Cardiogenic Congestion Disease Analysis v1

## S1 — 2026 ESC Heart Failure Guideline

Køber L, Adamo M, Ruwald AC, Tomasoni D, et al.  
**2026 ESC Guidelines for the management of heart failure.**  
European Heart Journal. Published 28 Aug 2026.  
PMID: 42661420  
DOI: 10.1093/eurheartj/ehag100  
URL: https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/advance-article/doi/10.1093/eurheartj/ehag100/8766302

Used for current diagnostic workflow, natriuretic-peptide interpretation, chest X-ray/echo roles, current HF terminology and phenotyping, and decompensated-HF diagnostic context.

## S2 — Second Universal Definition of Heart Failure 2026

Walsh MN, Kober L, Sliwa K, Adamo M, Agarwal A, et al.  
**AHA/ACC/ESC/WHF Expert Consensus Document: Second Universal Definition of Heart Failure (2026).**  
European Heart Journal. 2026;47(32):4357-4374.  
PMID: 42366993  
DOI: 10.1093/eurheartj/ehag500  
Parallel Circulation publication: PMID 42366997; DOI 10.1161/CIR.0000000000001455  
URL: https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/47/32/4357/8719720

Used for the core principle that HF is a clinical syndrome established by accumulation of compatible symptoms/signs and objective cardiac/congestion evidence rather than a single test. Also used for current stage/trajectory concepts and the warning that normal NP values can occur in some HFpEF patients.

## S3 — 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA Guideline

Heidenreich PA, Bozkurt B, Aguilar D, Allen LA, et al.  
**2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA Guideline for the Management of Heart Failure.**  
Circulation. 2022;145(18):e895-e1032.  
PMID: 35363499  
DOI: 10.1161/CIR.0000000000001063  
URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35363499/

Used for BNP/NT-proBNP diagnostic-support semantics, non-HF causes of elevated natriuretic peptides, chest radiography for heart size/congestion/alternative diagnoses, and echocardiography for cardiac structure/function.

## S4 — Pulmonary-congestion imaging study

Miger K, Olesen ASO, Grand J, Fabricius-Bjerre A, et al.  
**Computed tomography or chest X-ray to assess pulmonary congestion in dyspnoeic patients with acute heart failure.**  
ESC Heart Failure. 2024;11(2):1163-1173.  
PMID: 38279517  
PMCID: PMC10966239  
DOI: 10.1002/ehf2.14688  
URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38279517/

Used to reinforce that CXR can contribute to congestion assessment but is not a standalone definitive HF diagnostic test.

## Current-source precedence

When older phenotype conventions directly differ from 2026 sources, use S1/S2 for new inference. Preserve historical clinician-documented labels with provenance rather than silently rewriting them.
