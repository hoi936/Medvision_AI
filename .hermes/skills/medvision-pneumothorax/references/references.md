## Source S2

**Title:** British Thoracic Society Guideline for pleural disease  
**Journal:** Thorax  
**Year:** 2023  
**PMID:** 37553157 (summary article)  
**Full guideline:** Thorax. 2023;78(Suppl 3):s1  
**URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37553157/  
**Full text:** https://thorax.bmj.com/content/78/Suppl_3/s1  
**Evidence type:** Clinical practice guideline, GRADE methodology  
**Primary use:** Current adult spontaneous pneumothorax guidance.

### Facts used in skill
- Pneumothorax describes air in the pleural space.
- Spontaneous pneumothorax occurs without trauma or a causative medical intervention.
- Conservative management can be considered for asymptomatic or minimally symptomatic PSP without physiological compromise.
- Current management is not determined by radiographic size alone.
- Symptoms, physiological compromise, recurrence/history and tension context are clinically important.

## Source S3

**Title:** Joint ERS/EACTS/ESTS clinical practice guidelines on adults with spontaneous pneumothorax  
**Authors:** Walker S, Hallifax R, Ricciardi S, et al.  
**Journal:** European Respiratory Journal  
**Year:** 2024  
**PMID:** 38806203  
**DOI:** 10.1183/13993003.00797-2023  
**URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38806203/  
**Evidence type:** International clinical practice guideline; systematic search + GRADE  
**Primary use:** Current evidence-based management context for adult spontaneous pneumothorax.

### Facts used in skill
- Conditional recommendation for conservative care in minimally symptomatic, clinically stable PSP.
- Clinical stability and symptoms are central context.
- Recurrence prevention and persistent air leak are separate clinical management questions.
- Evidence gaps remain for some interventions.

# Citation policy for Hermes

Every medical statement added to `SKILL.md` should be traceable to one or more Source IDs:

- `[S1]`
- `[S2]`
- `[S3]`
- `[S4]`
- `[S5]`

If no source supports a proposed rule:

- do not add it as a medical fact;
- mark it as `EVIDENCE_GAP`;
- or explicitly label it as `MEDVISION_SYSTEM_POLICY` if it is a software/safety rule rather than medical knowledge.

# Update policy

When a newer guideline or consensus statement is added:
1. record publication year and scope;
2. compare against existing statements;
3. do not silently overwrite conflicting guidance;
4. document superseded rules;
5. update `SKILL.md` only after evidence review.
